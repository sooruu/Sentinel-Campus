The solution

One console for the perimeter.

Sentinel reads, checks, decides, records, and requests an opening pulse—while the gate controller remains responsible for physical safety.

07 / 10
Safety and privacy

The system fails safely.

Manual fallback

The physical button remains usable when software, network, or Pi power is unavailable.

No uncertain opening

Low-confidence, unknown, blocked, and stale reads stay closed.

Hardware interlock

Obstacle detection and emergency stop remain inside the gate controller.

Traceability

Decisions, overrides, and exported evidence are recorded for review.

The first pilot is one gate, supervised, in SHADOW mode before AUTO is enabled.

Safety is a boundary, not a UI featureSentinel Campus
